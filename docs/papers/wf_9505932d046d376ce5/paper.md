## 1. 这项工作研究什么
The RSI technical addendum proposes a bidirectional neuro-symbolic loop: latent representations are projected into a JSON-LD semantic hypergraph, candidate symbolic/AST changes are checked for noncontradiction by an SMT solver, and validated relations are re-embedded. Local cosine retrieval and remote hyperedge traversal are separate inference channels. Explicit RSI-concept bridge: symbolic validation and re-embedding are proposed to persistently reorganize representations for later inference. The broad software-tool cohort is reviewed only through its official attached technical addendum.
