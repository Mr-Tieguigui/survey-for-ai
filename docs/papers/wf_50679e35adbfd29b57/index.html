1. 这项工作研究什么

Reports a frozen finite symbolic lineage with persistent changes to acquisition machinery, matched predecessor controls, mutation/ablation, preserved bytes and separate project-authored checking. Two successive machinery generations expand reachable acquisition outcomes under the authored budget; a blind transfer test preserves a failed fixed-point certificate rather than treating every checker predicate as passed. These are bounded causal mechanism findings, not open-ended RSI. Direct bounded recursive modification of acquisition machinery, with explicit failure and transfer boundaries.
