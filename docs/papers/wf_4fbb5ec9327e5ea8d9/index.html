1. 这项工作研究什么

QHORYN/SELFWEAVE defines cross-generation improvability as a difference of future-probe treatment effects between pre-intervention arms. Its horizon-indexed kernel fixes intermediate policy, budgets, evaluator version and probe semantics; a positive entry means a current intervention improves the return to a specified later probe. Direct formal/prospective RSI measurement framework; synthetic/theoretical release, not confirmed real-agent acceleration.
