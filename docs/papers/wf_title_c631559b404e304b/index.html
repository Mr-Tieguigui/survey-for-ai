Connects noncommutation and frame-dependent identity to philosophical self-reference and differentiates ordinary modification from changing the discrimination apparatus. 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The Class A argument assumes an algebraic contrast-based self-model and active apparatus-changing updates, with exceptional cancellation cases. Other architectures lie outside its stated universality. It is theoretical/philosophical analysis, not empirical evidence that all RSI fails or that persistent goals are impossible.
