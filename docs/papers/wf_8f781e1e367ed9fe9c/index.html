6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Convergence depends on local smoothness and strong concavity, and evaluation can overfit the task distribution. Claimed sparsity efficiency and complexity reductions are not end-to-end hardware or capability measurements. The local iteration bound and numerical example are not reconciled here and are not certified as an acceleration law.
