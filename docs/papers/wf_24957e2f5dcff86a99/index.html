1. 这项工作研究什么

AutoPersonas models bounded feedback from assembled persona state to generated life material, recognized evidence and revised state; daily through long-horizon revisions govern current-state authority. The body reports a fixed-canon eight-model 40-day/200-event baseline stress test and a distinct semantic-theme ablation/full-runtime A/B. These are persona-state and environment evolution diagnostics, not a foundation-model training result. Direct bounded persistent persona/environment self-evolution; useful for state authority and fixation diagnostics, without evidence of recursive capability acceleration.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • One persona canon limits the quantitative baseline; semantic-theme metrics differ from the action taxonomy. Full-runtime comparisons use one run per condition without variance bands over independently sampled canons. Production prompts, ranking, schedules and raw logs are withheld, costs qualitative. Author-measured mechanism gains do not establish improved improvement machinery.
