## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- The endpoint is generated self-description, not internal experience, introspective correctness or an improving successor. Steering tests three models at one layer/coefficient; experiential voice evidence is partly behavioral, and the exact template circuit is not traced. An imperfect Qwen random-direction control limits specificity.
