1. 这项工作研究什么

The released AxiomGraph Digital NeuroGenesis document specifies a cognitive graph of functional nodes and weighted/trust/cost edges, a genome defining growth/pruning permissions and a persistent developmental ledger. Its loop scores task outcomes, strengthens or prunes pathways, stores experience and records architectural change. Explicit architectural RSI bridge: evaluation is intended to produce persistent graph growth/pruning and future routing changes. Conceptual development rules must be separated from empirical capability gain.
