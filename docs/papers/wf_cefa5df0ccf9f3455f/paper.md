## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- The illustrative experiment exhaustively enumerates a two-state two-symbol restricted machine with fixed 12-bit programs/tape and 12 iterations. Self-replicating attractors in this model are not empirical AI capability improvement or a universal life-inevitability result.
