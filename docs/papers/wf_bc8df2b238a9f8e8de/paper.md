## 1. 这项工作研究什么
FELIX reports organisms that process sequential data, track energy and mutate/reproduce/transfer structure under metabolic selection. Fitness is prediction-loss reduction per consumed energy; retained offspring, directed transfer and displacement propagate structural variants, and generational comparison uses best post-warmup rolling sustained fitness. Direct bounded RSI bridge: inherited structure and mutation/selection affect descendants' learning efficiency. Three reported generations do not establish unlimited improvement or acceleration.

Architecture, mutation operators and hyperparameters are undisclosed and no code is released; wider task generalization, deeper chains and scaling remain unknown. Reproducibility in the title is an author claim, not an independent replication here.
