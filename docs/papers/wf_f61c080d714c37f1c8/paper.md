## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- Soundness depends on model assumptions and a valid upper Lipschitz bound; the GPT-2 estimate is not an analytic certificate. Fifty inside-ball samples do not prove zero unsafe acceptance generally. Safe modifications/TPR are not useful capability improvements. Separable, discrete, preverified or short finite regimes are qualified exceptions. Companion results and unchecked proof/numeric assertions are not imported.
