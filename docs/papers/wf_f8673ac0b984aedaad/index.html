1. 这项工作研究什么

Capability-Delta Evaluation separates retaining a self-modification from inheriting production authorization. Definition4.4 allows retention under an independently chosen criterion but requires reauthorization when a sound over-approximation of newly reachable states intersects an externally defined material-consequence set. Direct formal RSI evaluation/governance proposal; distinct authorization gate, not an empirically validated general reachability solver.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Exact reachability may be intractable/uncomputable; a loose sound approximation can trigger reauthorization on every change. Materiality is supplied externally, environmental affordances may be unmodeled, and external adversaries may extend reach beyond the cooperative model.
