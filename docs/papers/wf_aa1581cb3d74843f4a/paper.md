## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- The protocol deliberately does not evaluate performance or usefulness. Its rule-mutation criterion is stronger than ordinary bounded improvement and must not be used to deny feedback-driven gains merely because an update rule or admissible class is fixed.
