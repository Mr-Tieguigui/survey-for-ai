Models hierarchical verification budgets with a second-order recurrence and tests its golden-ratio interpretation against analytical single-level and cascaded speculative-decoding cost models. Reports a negative structural fit: these decoding models use first-order contraction rather than the required two-generation memory. The introduction explicitly bridges generator-verifier allocation to RSI; retained as a bounded critique of a universal ratio conjecture.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The parameter sweep evaluates a published cost formula, not deployed language-model timing or autonomous RSI. The recurrence requires genuine second-order dependence and stationary inheritance; whether a deployed system realizes these assumptions remains open. Its negative result does not rule out ordinary self-improvement.
