## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- Fitness need not improve without additional conditions; fitness is not necessarily utility. Fixed tree assumptions exclude recombination, population-conditioned descendant design and strategic interactions. These are model results, not empirical autonomous design gains or universal acceleration.
