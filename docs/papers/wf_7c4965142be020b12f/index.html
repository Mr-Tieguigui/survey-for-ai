6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Feedback alignment and sufficient coverage remain necessary; aligned feedback alone does not ensure discovery. The game-generating meta-game lacks a well-defined inner-style feedback mechanism, and preservation of coverage and alignment is open. This is a research agenda, not a demonstrated boundless learner.
