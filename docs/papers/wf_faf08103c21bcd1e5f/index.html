1. 这项工作研究什么

EXPAI is a conceptual class of experience-based recursive self-improvers, with small tentative knowledge/skill granules rated by predictive success, used for subgoal generation only after experiential support, and pruned under memory constraints. It replaces proof of every candidate benefit with fine-grained performance rating and discusses repeated stakeholder testing during growth. Explicit RSI theory bridge: current granules guide experiments and acquisition of later granules while a fixed controller participates in positive feedback. Fixed controller does not negate that bounded feedback, but the conceptual argument is not an empirical safety or acceleration guarantee.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论
