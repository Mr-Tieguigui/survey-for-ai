1. 这项工作研究什么

The ASI treatise proposes a dynamical-spectral self-model: estimate local geometry/Jacobian structure, compare affinity with target stable subspaces, and navigate or reshape internal landscapes through controlled changes of cost functions and representation. It explicitly links this to a geometric form of recursive self-modification. Explicit conceptual RSI bridge: the system is intended to inspect its own cognitive landscape and alter the structure shaping future computation. Philosophical integration is not evidence of autonomous capability acceleration.
