## 1. 这项工作研究什么
SO-RSI augments ordinary workflow revision with detection of recurrence, reversal and expectation mismatch, evidence-seeking mechanism investigation, and intervention patches. Persistent investigation records connect hypotheses, checks and open questions; revised workflows return to ordinary outer validation, and only accepted edits change the active system. Direct RSI bridge: diagnosis alters the workflow generating future task solutions, accepted patches persist, and investigation memory is reused across rounds. Detector calls and investigations consume the shared research budget.
