6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The paper disavows claiming Gödelian difficulty blocks AI or proposing this formalism for actual AI. It begins with known crisp deterministic environments; rational coherence remains partial and probabilistic/bounded decision criteria remain open. The probabilistic extension cannot satisfactorily represent ordinary evidence. No practical RSI guarantee or rollout is inferred.
