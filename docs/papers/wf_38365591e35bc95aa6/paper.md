## 1. 这项工作研究什么
Runesmith separates proposing instruments from a deterministic authority/evidence boundary. The runtime describes bounded episodes and outcome-linked learning, while the released Core uses repair organs, Kaizen diagnosis/development replay, keep-best logging and explicit trial activation or rejection. Direct bounded RSI bridge: persistent repair code, memory and policy can be revised from evaluated outcomes, with promotion/rollback outside the proposer. Repeated episodes or model calls alone are not learning.

## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- The report distinguishes the proposed runtime from its narrower released Core. Internal reviews and recomputations share the project/model lineage; private task pools and nonhermetic evaluators constrain reproducibility. No general convergence, safety or semantic-completeness guarantee is asserted.
