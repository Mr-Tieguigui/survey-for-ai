## 6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

- At the objective-symbol level reactions are automatic and alternative selection or offline mental phenomena require additional mechanisms. The selected test guidelines describe organization/homeostatic preservation under perturbations, not benchmarked recursive enhancement of an AI improver.
