1. 这项工作研究什么

MASSIF defines hidden-state directional observables and a persistence-flip/lead-lag taxonomy, then applies a lexical-repetition stress protocol to 13 model architectures. This is diagnostic evaluation rather than a survey or self-improvement training method.   • The source restricts findings to lexical repetition, models at most 4B and laboratory tests; semantic recursion and natural prompting are untested, and alignment is not a causal guarantee or deployment certification.
