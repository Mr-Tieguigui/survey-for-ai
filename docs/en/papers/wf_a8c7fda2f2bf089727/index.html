1. 这项工作研究什么

EVE-SYNRIEL WCRC tracks externally acquired witness roots and derived-artifact dependencies, preserves set-valued prediction patterns for decision-equivalent hypotheses, and constructs costed experimental witness codes. A compiler library stores checked rules/artifacts while protected admission prevents candidates from redefining evidence or success criteria. RSI-infrastructure bridge: witness/provenance memory and compiled decisions may be reused to reduce later improvement work under protected contracts. A convincing recursive result would still require generations outperforming frozen-improver controls on new tasks.
