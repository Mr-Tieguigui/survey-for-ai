6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Only four questions per group, one model family/temperature and English are tested. LLM claim compression and embedding similarity may bias the metric; embedding proximity is not logical equivalence. The design cannot distinguish mechanisms, and response instability establishes neither consciousness nor reliable self-improvement feedback.
