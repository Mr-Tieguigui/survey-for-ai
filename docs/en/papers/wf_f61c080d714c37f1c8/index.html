1. 这项工作研究什么

The paper formulates binary mutation safety gates using cumulative false-acceptance/true-positive conditions. The Hölder–Rényi theorem assumes overlapping feature distributions, finite divergence and a specified power-law risk schedule, contrasting a constructive Lipschitz-ball verifier. GPT-2 LoRA validation defines safety through held-out perplexity and uses finite-difference estimated Lipschitz bounds with a safety multiplier. Direct conditional safety-gate limitation/proposal, with a restricted perplexity-defined mutation assay rather than sustained beneficial RSI.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Soundness depends on model assumptions and a valid upper Lipschitz bound; the GPT-2 estimate is not an analytic certificate. Fifty inside-ball samples do not prove zero unsafe acceptance generally. Safe modifications/TPR are not useful capability improvements. Separable, discrete, preverified or short finite regimes are qualified exceptions. Companion results and unchecked proof/numeric assertions are not imported.
