1. 这项工作研究什么

The audit prototype computes sharp feasible FPR/recall bounds over all binary completions of unknown labels in a declared fixed pool, returning PASS/REJECT/DEFER. Candidate hashes, policy epochs and scope bind activation receipts. Its two-epoch micro pilot freezes model proposals before calibration and separates policy-derived oracle labels from candidate firing. Direct evidence/admission governance for coding-agent rule updates; only the selected finite-pool and micro-pilot claims are admitted.
