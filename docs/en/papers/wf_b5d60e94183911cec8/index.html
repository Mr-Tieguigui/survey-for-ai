1. 这项工作研究什么

Defines a coupled observer/update loop and an external entropy sink with independent grounding, discrepancy computation and corrective injection. Argues that closed inference cannot gain new information about an external reference and motivates continuing human oversight. This is conceptual/formal analysis, not an evaluated RSI implementation. Direct theoretical discussion of grounding and oversight of recursive inference; does not establish a universal impossibility of ordinary bounded self-improvement.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The appendix explicitly separates non-increasing mutual information from any theorem of monotonically increasing error or drift. The bandwidth law is conjectural and its scalar spectral-radius characterization is acknowledged as incomplete. Human exclusivity and practical stability are not independently established here.
