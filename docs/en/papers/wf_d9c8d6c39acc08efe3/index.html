6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Sampling is nonrandom/unrepresentative; one author developed codes without inter-rater reliability. Findings are time-specific descriptive beliefs, explicitly not researcher consensus. Expectations of speedups/explosion are not measured productivity, validated technical forecasts or implemented RSI. Earlier v2 abstract history remains separate.
