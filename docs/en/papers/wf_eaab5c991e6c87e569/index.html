1. 这项工作研究什么

Redwood describes a specification-driven platform that generates architecture, RTL, verification collateral, firmware and kernels, with humans maintaining the platform and adjusting design intent from functional/PPA feedback. The paper also reports deploying Qwen3 on the resulting accelerator and using its repeated samples for timing and kernel improvements to a future accelerator generation. Explicit hardware RSI bridge: compute built through the AI design process hosts a model whose outputs feed later hardware/kernel design. Retain this bounded case without equating an automated chip-design loop with open-ended self-modification.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The human-written specification and maintained platform remain inputs to the design process. The body distinguishes measured FPGA operation from Samsung 8 nm projections; physical design, tapeout and post-silicon validation are future work. The deployment-to-redesign example does not prove sustained recursive acceleration.
