6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The obstruction requires that precise same-run causal path and gives a worst-case adversarial instance, not failure of typical predictive control. Resource/noise estimates depend on assumptions. No self-improving AI implementation or general RSI impossibility is established.
