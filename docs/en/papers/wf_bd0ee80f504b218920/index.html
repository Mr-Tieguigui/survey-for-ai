1. 这项工作研究什么

EvoUndo keeps capability-positive forward mutations immutable while repairing witness/recovery programs and effect contracts. Typed observational equivalence, tracked effects, four-attempt synthesis and Wilson-LCB admission govern round trips. The 197 natural-failure factorial uses 10 development/40 hidden counterfactuals with aggregate hidden feedback; a separate 300-task holdout is feedback-isolated. Direct recoverability governance for bounded persistent harness mutation; distinguish fixed-mutation recovery repair from optimizing forward changes or proving universal rollback.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • Constructive-oracle strata use privileged hidden/pre-state access retrospectively, not a deployed synthesizer. Recovery-language completeness and joint forward-mutation optimization are untested. Effect-scoped snapshots win when affected pre-state is known/serializable; distributed, API, physical/financial effects are outside exact restoration. WAL/ARIES was not implemented.
