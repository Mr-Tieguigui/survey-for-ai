1. 这项工作研究什么

VFO proposes the task-level Verifier Coverage Index VCI=validity×cheapness×timeliness×robustness. It appends generation-specific task success to a fixed task matrix, refits generation-by-VCI improvement slopes and reports coverage/floor series; its preregistered tripwire compares low-VCI with high-VCI improvement rates. Direct proposed RSI measurement instrument; retain prospective protocol separately from executed empirical evidence.
