1. 这项工作研究什么

EVE–COVARA v2 reports a bounded six-gene optimizer/proposal-law search on8-dimensional quadratic tasks. Each round screens4 proposals on64 tasks and admits a candidate using4,096 fresh paired tasks; separate audits do not feed selection. It compares recursive mutation, frozen mutation and random search across3 seeds/100 rounds. Direct bounded empirical RSI-related mutation-law update with mixed comparative evidence; retain author-reported results and exclude claims of open-ended acceleration.
