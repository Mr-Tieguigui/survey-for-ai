1. 这项工作研究什么

Bounded Gödelian Language Models proposes typed, sandboxed child updates admitted only after formula-bound symbolic proofs, a separate statistical certificate and rollback verification. A quality/diversity archive distinguishes research, certified and deployed states; persistent meta changes remain subject to the external gate. Direct formal RSI bridge: children and proposal-process changes may affect future updates, while a trusted certificate/ledger/installer boundary remains fixed. Conceptual architecture is not empirical G3 achievement.

6. 局限、冲突与不能推出的结论

  • The reported synthetic experiment tests one Bernoulli survival gate. No trained multi-generation LLM lineage, real-executor proof overhead or calibrated risk/ambiguity experiments are reported. Proofs apply only to encoded properties and environment assumptions, not unrestricted language behavior.
